Good to Know

Frequently asked

How do I book an appointment?

Complete the booking form above and WhatsApp opens with your details ready to send. We confirm your slot within the hour during opening times.

Do you offer a consultation before colour?

Yes — a complimentary consultation is included with every colour service so we can agree shade, budget and maintenance up front.

How long does balayage take?

Most balayage appointments take 3 to 4 hours, depending on hair length, density and starting colour.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel. Late cancellations may incur a fee of 50% of the service.

Do you style bridal parties?

Absolutely. We offer trials, on-site morning-of styling and a private suite for groups of up to 8.
